Now you know how everything works and we can start a test round. 

You will have 20 seconds to shoot as many balloons as possible. 
The balloons will spawn 360° around you. 
If you hit the green balloons early you get +3, otherwise +1. 
If you hit the blue balloons you get -5. 

Try to collect as many points as possible. 

Press Trackpad to start the test round.
