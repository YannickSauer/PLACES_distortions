Now, the experiment starts!

The experiment divides into 3 phases: 
1. Pre Adaptation Phase: 12 rounds of 5 motion assessment test and 20 secs of balloon shooting game. 
2. Adaptation Phase: 20 rounds of 45 secs of balloon shooting game. 
3. Post Adaptation Phase: 12 rounds of 5 motion assessment test and 20 secs of balloon shooting game. 

Each phase takes about 15 minutes.
