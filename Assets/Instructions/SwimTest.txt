In the movement assessment tests you have to judge if your environment moves naturally/stable or unnaturally/unstable.
For this task we will show you a random dot pattern. These dots are representing a wall in front of you. This means that the dots are projected onto this wall. The wall itself will not be visible.  
You will keep your eyes on a red fixation dot and shake your head as you have learned before. 
After shaking the head twice to both sides you return to the middle and the dots disappear. 
Now, you have to assess whether the environment aka the dots were moving naturally/stable or unnaturally/unstable. 
If you think the dots moved naturally/stable, you pull the right hand trigger.
If you think the dots moved unnaturally/unstable, you pull the left hand trigger.
