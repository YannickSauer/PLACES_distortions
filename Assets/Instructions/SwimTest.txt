In the movement assessment tests, you'll judge if your environment moves naturally/stable or unnaturally/unstable.
For this task we will show you a <b>random dot pattern</b>. These dots are <b>representing a wall</b> in front of you. This means that the dots are projected onto this wall. The wall itself will not be visible.  

Keep your eyes on the red fixation dot and shake your head as you learned before.  
After turning your head twice to each side and returning to center, the dots will disappear. 

Then, decide if the dots moved <color=#FF00FF>naturally/stable</color> or <color=#00FFFF>unnaturally/unstable</color>.

If you think the dots moved <color=#FF00FF>naturally/stable</color>, you pull the <color=#FF00FF>right</color> hand trigger.
If you think the dots moved <color=#00FFFF>unnaturally/unstable</color>, you pull the <color=#00FFFF>left</color> hand trigger.

<b>Press the trackpad to start.</b>
